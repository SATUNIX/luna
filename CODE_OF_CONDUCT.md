# Code of conduct

Luna (`pi-system`) adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and any other channel where you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and accept review of your work
gracefully. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing someone
else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a concern **privately**. Open an issue that says only that you have a conduct report, with
no detail and no names, and a maintainer will arrange a private channel. A report about a
maintainer is handled by a different maintainer. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the
Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
